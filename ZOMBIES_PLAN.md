# Kennerz Zombies Mode: plan

The zombies mode mod (com.kennerz.zombies, "Kennerz-Zombies"): the hub that ties Ray Gun, Thundergun, PaP and Perk Beverages together. Plan moved here from `C:\Git\99_Tools\CONTEXT.md` (2026-10-31). Nothing below is built yet except the gun logger (see "Tools now").

**HARD RULE (user, 2026-11-01): nothing of the zombies mode may affect the normal game.** Players install the mod, play EFT as usual, and play zombies whenever they like: every mechanic (slaps, points, ammo rules, handling, box, the gun hand-over and replacing, weight/armour rules...) is only switched on inside a zombies match. Test tools that work in normal raids (e.g. the F12 raid box) are for development only and must be gone or hidden behind a debug switch in releases.

**COMPATIBILITY GOAL (user, 2026-11-01):** people will run the zombies mode inside serious playthroughs with the common big mods installed. It must work alongside them and never need anyone to change their setup; fixes always go in our mod (never the other mod, its config or load order). Test targets (from the user's mod list): SAIN, Fika, Questing Bots, Looting Bots, MoreBots (bot mods: when building the zombie waves), SVM (Server Value Modifier) and UI Fixes (when doing the loadout swap, inventory lock and HUD). Read their sources from `C:\Git\2_References` when available.

## Plan

- A "Zombies" switch in the top right of the map select. Picking it and loading a map (e.g. Customs) starts a zombies match instead of a normal raid: a plain character with the M1911 as the starter gun, the original-style.
- After a zombies match the player earns a new currency, **ZM coins**, based on what they did and how well (rounds, kills etc.; formula to be decided).
- ZM coins buy **perk upgrades** (stronger versions of each perk's effect, like Cold War's perk tiers).
- The upgrade UI opens by interacting with the **Wunderfizz machine from Cold War Zombies** (the user may extract it; the original's `p7_zm_vending_wonder` is in Greyhound too).
- Ties in with the earlier idea of perk machines in raids during zombies mode.
- How it would be built (assessed 2026-10-31): EFT's own Halloween **infected** bots are the zombies (SPT 4.1.6 still has the infection code). Customs stays Customs: at raid start the mod sets out a placement list (perk machines, wall buys, box, barricades, invisible walls round the Stronghold play area), as the hideout machines are; Unity Explorer edits don't save, it's only for finding positions. Normal bot waves off, extracts off, round loop + wave spawning client side. The loadout swap must never risk the real PMC stash (model it on the scav raid's separate character). Performance: fewer, simpler bots than a SAIN raid; Customs still renders as normal. Build order: infected-only waves + round counter prototype, then the toggle and loadout, then the placement list, then ZM coins / Wunderfizz.
- **Health = slaps, not EFT HP (draft rules):** zombie hits are caught before EFT applies them and counted as slaps (no bleeds, fractures or limb damage). Base 2 slaps (2nd = down), Jugg +3 (5). No slap for ~4 s refills them. Down = last stand: forced prone, pistol only, ~30-45 s bleed-out; revive (team, or Quick Revive self-revive solo) or the match ends and ZM coins are paid.
- **Armour (Cold War style):** a zombies-only PACA with 1 plate slot; each plate takes one slap before health and stays broken; an in-match armour station adds slots (1-3) and refills plates. Plates are counted by the mod (the vest is the look). HUD: plate pips over slap pips. The PACA is the set rig every player wears (user, 2026-11-01). Plates and upgrades will be bought with a **scrap** system (Cold War's salvage), designed later.
- **Arcade feel:** EFT's top-left health display hidden in the mode; carried weight counts as 0 (always naked speed); armour and gun speed/ergo penalties zeroed. Stamina: keep a sprint limit so Stamin-Up matters (ask the user before making it infinite).
- **Slap feedback:** a hit sound, a quick camera-only jolt that springs back (EFT's flinch moves the gun; ours must not touch aim), red screen edges that deepen with each slap held, the original's red veiny overlay + heavy breathing one slap from down, all fading as slaps regen. Look for the original's blood/vein overlay images in the GrayWolf export first.
- **The original points** in-match (kills, hits) pay for wall buys, perks, plates, doors; ZM coins are the between-matches currency.
- **Points and ammo (user's design):** hit 10, kill 100, knife kill 150; prone at a perk machine = 100, once per machine per player. Preset guns, one spare mag, a reserve shown the original-style `30 / 240`. Every reload is EFT's quick reload (the mag drops like the original); the mod picks the dropped mag up, puts it back in the hidden rig and refills it to `min(mag, left + reserve)`, rounds left in it go back to the reserve (reload at 12 = 18 from the reserve). The inventory is locked for the whole match. Points/reserve: the user first said roubles + real bullets in the hidden inventory; Claude recommended plain counters (nothing to leak into the stash, easy to sync for Fika). Decision pending. Check early that EFT's quick reload copes with the spare mag being put back by code; fallback: the mod does the reload and only plays the quick-reload animation.
- **Loadout: never at risk (planned):** the server mod saves the PMC's whole equipment to its own storage with a "zombies match running" marker, puts in the zombies loadout (plain character, preset M1911, hidden rig) and the client loads that. At the end SPT's normal end-of-raid handling is skipped for zombies raids (nothing from the raid is applied, no death loss or insurance) and the backup goes back exactly. On a crash, the marker makes the server restore the backup at next login before anything else. Players bring nothing back.
- **ZM coins:** at match end the client sends a summary (round, kills, headshots, knife kills, points, revives, time, XP); the server works out the coins with a formula in `config.json` (round weighs most) and adds them to a ZM coin balance in the mod's storage (not roubles); the original-style end screen with the breakdown. Open: whether a zombies match also gives normal PMC XP.
- **Zombie health and gun damage (proposed):** The original's curve: round 1 = 150 HP, +100 per round to round 9 (950), then x1.1 per round (~2.5k at 20, ~6.5k at 30, ~45k at 50). The original has no cap; an optional cap round in config (user to decide). Damage per *gun*, not per bullet: in zombies mode hits on zombies are caught (like slaps) and a weapon table in config gives base damage + head/torso/limb multipliers (the original heads ~2-4x); each zombie has one health number (ours, not EFT's limbs). PaP in zombies mode = a big multiplier (~2x+, via PaP's API; normal PaP stays 1.17). Ray Gun high flat + splash, Thundergun one-shots its cone until very high rounds, Insta-Kill ignores health, knife flat (strong early, useless late). Hit/kill points paid from the same hook.
- **Handling (user, 2026-10-31):** zombies guns kick less and scatter less than EFT as standard (start: recoil ~60%, spread ~half); PaP turns them into laser beams (recoil ~20%, spread near 0). Only in a zombies match; normal raids and normal PaP untouched. Recoil scaled the way PaP already scales it; spread via EFT's accuracy (MOA). One standard round per calibre (ammo sets bullet speed/drop only, not damage or extra spread). All numbers in the zombies config. Doesn't touch where you aim.
